Where do I start?

Going solo: single men.

Las Vegas has plenty to do solo, but lifestyle venues often have special rules and pricing for single men. Knowing that before you arrive makes the night much easier.

A good starting point

Pick the kind of experience you actually want—not the most extreme option you can find. Las Vegas gives you room to move gradually from playful and cultural to social and lifestyle-focused.

Good fits

  • Erotic Heritage Museum
  • Traditional adult nightlife
  • AVN Expo
  • Lifestyle clubs that explicitly admit solo men

A simple progression

  1. Start low-pressure: museum, show or adult shopping.
  2. If you want nightlife, choose a venue that clearly welcomes solo guests.
  3. For lifestyle venues, verify that night’s solo-male policy and pricing.
  4. Arrive social, respectful and without expectations of participation.
Important

Do not assume every lifestyle party admits solo men. Couples-only and couples-plus-women events are common.
