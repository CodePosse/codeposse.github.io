Where do I start?

LGBTQ+ Vegas after dark.

Adult Las Vegas includes men’s spas, trans-inclusive nightlife, museums, conventions and mainstream venues that work well for LGBTQ+ visitors.

A good starting point

Pick the kind of experience you actually want—not the most extreme option you can find. Las Vegas gives you room to move gradually from playful and cultural to social and lifestyle-focused.

Good fits

  • Entourage Vegas
  • Club Trans-Action
  • Erotic Heritage Museum
  • AVN / specialty events

A simple progression

  1. Decide whether you want community/social space, nightlife, culture or an event.
  2. Check current audience and identity policies directly with the venue.
  3. Use public attractions as easy group meetup points.
  4. Verify event location and schedule on the day you go.
Important

Venue policies and audience focus vary significantly. A place being adult-oriented does not automatically mean it is the right LGBTQ+ environment for you.
