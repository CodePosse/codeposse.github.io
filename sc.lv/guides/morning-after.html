The Morning After

Whatever happened last night, breakfast still exists. Here’s how to keep the weekend alive when everyone is operating at about 63%.

Start softer

Day two does not need to compete with night one. Pick something lower-pressure: a museum, a spa appointment, a pool day, a long brunch, shopping or simply an attraction where nobody has to make another major decision.

Good recovery-day moves

  • Erotic Heritage Museum: adult-themed, interesting and very easy to do at your own pace.
  • Adult shopping: surprisingly good as a low-commitment couples or group activity.
  • Spa / pool time: choose conventional or clothing-optional depending on the group’s remaining ambition.
  • Late brunch: hydrate, debrief and establish which stories are now classified.
