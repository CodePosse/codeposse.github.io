Where do I start?

The crew: guys’ night.

A group of guys looking for adult Vegas fun is usually better served by group-friendly nightlife, shows, conventions and attractions than by lifestyle clubs with restrictive solo-male policies.

A good starting point

Pick the kind of experience you actually want—not the most extreme option you can find. Las Vegas gives you room to move gradually from playful and cultural to social and lifestyle-focused.

Good fits

  • Mansion / adult nightlife
  • Erotic Heritage Museum
  • AVN Expo
  • Traditional gentlemen’s clubs

A simple progression

  1. Pick a group-friendly anchor activity.
  2. Reserve transportation or a table/package only after comparing the terms.
  3. Keep the group together without pressuring everyone into the same activity.
  4. Use lifestyle venues only when the published admission policy clearly fits your group.
Important

Do not plan a group night around a lifestyle club unless the venue explicitly allows your group. Many lifestyle spaces limit or prohibit groups of solo men.
