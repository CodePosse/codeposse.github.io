Where do I start?

Going solo: single women.

Solo women often have more access options than solo men, but the best choice depends on whether you want a museum, nightlife, a spa, a social event or a lifestyle venue.

A good starting point

Pick the kind of experience you actually want—not the most extreme option you can find. Las Vegas gives you room to move gradually from playful and cultural to social and lifestyle-focused.

Good fits

  • Erotic Heritage Museum
  • Sea Mountain Spa
  • Swingers Circle
  • Lifestyle clubs with solo-woman admission

A simple progression

  1. Choose your comfort level: attraction, nightlife, clothing-optional or lifestyle.
  2. Check whether reservations or memberships are required.
  3. Tell a friend where you are going and keep your own transportation option.
  4. Remember that admission never implies participation.
Important

Do not let “women welcome” substitute for your own safety and comfort check. Transportation, exit plans and venue reputation still matter.
